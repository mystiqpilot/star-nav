When the dish has no GNSS fix of its own (Starlink only), apps get an honest accuracy of at least 85 m instead of the dish's few metres, and the same when the phone's own GPS is distrusted. When the dish refuses position requests, Star Nav names the reason and uses the position from the dish's diagnostics instead. Fixed: the position's age is measured against GNSS time; a mount offset 180° wrong is flipped.
